About the brand

Built from beach memories, color ideas, and a young founder’s imagination.

Seaside Serenity began with hand-drawn pages full of product names, prices, category ideas, a tagline, and a sunrise logo concept. This version keeps that energy visible while presenting it in a cleaner public story.

public founder note

Hi, I’m Lillian.

I created Seaside Serenity because I love the beach, bright colors, and designing clothes that feel fun and peaceful. This collection is inspired by sunshine, ocean colors, and ideas that started as sketches on paper.

I wanted the brand to feel cheerful, calm, creative, and a little dreamy — like carrying a bit of summer with you.

What stayed from the original drawings

  • The exact Seaside Serenity name and tagline.
  • A sun / sunrise logo motif with rounded boutique styling.
  • Category labels like hoodies, sweaters, PJs, tote bag, swimwear, and special.
  • Named products with visible prices and playful motifs.

Creative principles

The brand should feel sunny, calm, and playful.

Sunny

Yellow, sunrise notes, soft coral, and warm highlights should make the site feel bright before it says a word.

Calm

Sea blue, airy spacing, and rounded forms keep it soft and peaceful instead of loud or chaotic.

Playful

Hearts, bows, fish, dots, and handwritten cues make it feel young without turning it into a generic kids template.

What is public

First name only, brand story, creative direction, and product concepts.

What stays parent-managed

Contact, payments, customer service, policies, shipping, and any eventual Shopify checkout.

Why this matters

The website can feel real and exciting for her without pushing adult business responsibilities onto her.
