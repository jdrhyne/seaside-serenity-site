About Seaside Serenity

A bright little brand with a calm coastal spirit.

Seaside Serenity began with a young imagination, a love of the beach, and a feeling that clothes could be cheerful, peaceful, and full of personality. Today the brand is growing into a parent-managed collection shaped by sunshine, ocean color, and playful detail.

founder note

Hi, I’m Lillian.

I started Seaside Serenity because I love the beach, bright colors, and clothes that feel happy and calm at the same time.

I wanted each piece to feel like a little bit of sunshine you can wear, with soft color, playful details, and a peaceful mood.

What defines the brand

  • Sun-washed color and a calm coastal palette.
  • Easy pieces that feel bright, wearable, and full of charm.
  • Playful details like bows, dots, fish, and sunrise motifs.
  • A boutique feel that stays warm, polished, and imaginative.

How it should feel

Sunny, calm, and playful from the first glance.

Sunny

Warm yellow, sunrise color, and soft coral should make Seaside Serenity feel bright before it says a word.

Calm

Sea blue, rounded forms, and airy space keep the brand soft and peaceful instead of loud or chaotic.

Playful

Bows, fish, dots, and handwritten touches keep it youthful and full of charm without feeling messy.

Inspired by

Beach days, ocean color, sunshine, and the feeling of summer staying a little longer.

Made to feel

Cheerful, peaceful, and easy to wear, with enough personality to stand out in a gentle way.

Family-managed

Seaside Serenity is parent-managed, with store operations, support, and future orders handled by a parent.
