FAQ

Built to be safe, simple, and parent-managed.

Is this a live store yet?

Not yet. This first build is a launch-ready brand site and collection preview. Checkout can be added through Shopify once products and print-on-demand vendors are finalized.

Why not launch everything at once?

Because the first win is quality. A tight first drop gives her something real to react to without dragging the family into complexity too early.

How is privacy handled?

The public story uses first name only. Parent-managed email, payments, customer communication, and any future checkout stay under adult control.
