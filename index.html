FAQ

A few things to know.

Can I order from the first collection now?

Not yet. The first collection is being prepared now, and online ordering is not open yet. You can browse the collection and explore each style while final launch details are being finalized.

What is in the first collection?

The first Seaside Serenity collection includes six pieces: Seaside Sunset Tee, Yellow Sun Tee, White Polka Dots Tee, Catamaran Sunrise Hoodie, Gray Bow Sweatshirt, and Fish Out of the Water Tote.

Who handles orders and support?

Seaside Serenity is parent-managed. Orders, customer support, policies, and store operations are handled by a parent.
